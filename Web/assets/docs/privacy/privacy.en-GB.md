# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to **Known and its platform editions** (Android, HarmonyOS, Windows desktop, hereinafter referred to as "the app"), developed and maintained by the project contributors. It explains how the app handles data, where data is stored, what may be shared, and the rights you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 No Personal Data Collected by Default

By default, the app does **not actively collect, upload, or transmit your personal information**, including your name, phone number, email address, ID number, geographic location, photo library, camera, microphone, or other sensitive personal information.

All data generated by the app, such as records, settings, and user content, is stored **locally on your device** by default and is never uploaded automatically to any external server.

### 1.2 Data from Optional Features

1. **Manual sync / backup**: if you manually enable export or backup, or configure a sync service yourself, the data is saved to a location you choose and is never uploaded automatically. Sync services are set up or chosen by you, and you are responsible for their security.
2. **Crash and error information**: the app does not report crash logs automatically. If you manually copy an error message and submit it to GitHub Issues or another feedback channel, the logs, screenshots, and error details you voluntarily provide are used only for troubleshooting and fixes. Logs may contain local file paths, so please redact private content before submitting.

### 1.3 Third-Party Components and Services

Each platform edition of the app is built on open-source technologies and does not include advertising or usage analytics SDKs (**except for the Android edition described below**). Some system capabilities are subject to the policies of the corresponding operating system and its provider.

The Android edition integrates **Google AdMob advertising and Firebase services** (used for crash reporting and product analytics). The related data is processed by Google under the Google privacy policy and the relevant terms. For details, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

The Windows desktop edition is built on WinUI3 and other open-source dependencies, and contains no advertising or usage analytics components.

The HarmonyOS edition is built on ArkTS and native system capabilities, which are subject to the policies of Huawei and its system services.

> The operating system itself may collect system-level information under its provider's policies. This behaviour is beyond the control of the app; please refer to the official privacy policy of your operating system.

## 2. How Information Is Used

1. Your local data, such as records and settings, is **used only on your device** to run the app's features and is never transmitted externally;
2. Issues, feedback, and error information you voluntarily submit to the developers are used only to locate bugs and improve this open-source project;
3. We will **never sell, rent, or distribute** any of your personal data.

## 3. Information Storage and Security

1. All of your app data is stored locally on your device by default. The storage location is determined by the app and the system, and you can export, back up, or delete the data yourself at any time;
2. The developers do **not access or retrieve** any data on your local device;
3. Open-source software cannot be absolutely secure, so please keep local backups of your data. If you transfer data over a network (for example, through a sync service you set up), be aware that network transmission carries security risks.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We will not share your information except in the following cases:

1. You explicitly make it public yourself (for example, content you submit publicly in a GitHub Issue);
2. It is required by law or requested by public authorities through legal procedures;
3. It involves third-party components or services the project depends on, whose behaviour is governed by their own terms and privacy rules.

## 5. Your Rights

- Access, modify, export, and delete all of your app data directly on your device;
- Stop using the app at any time, after which no further data processing takes place;
- Raise privacy questions by opening an Issue in the project's GitHub repository.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for students, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. Minors should use the app with the knowledge and company of a guardian. If you believe a minor's personal information has been submitted through the app, please open a GitHub Issue to request its removal, and we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Third Parties and External Links

No platform edition of the app contains built-in advertising (for advertising in the Android edition, see section 1.3 above). The project's code repository and documentation are hosted on GitHub, and visiting the GitHub website is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the open-source project evolves. Updated versions are published together with the project repository, and you can visit the repository to read the latest version. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated privacy policy.
